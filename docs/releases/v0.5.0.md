**Not published.** This is a proposed minor collection release, not a tag, release, or claim of completed release qualification. Published v0.4.1 and all stable installation defaults remain unchanged until separately approved publication and public tag/archive verification. This preparation creates no tag or release.

The reviewed implementation baseline is the merged experiment-design implementation. The final preparation commit/tree and archive identities belong in the external verification record, not in this file. The complete release scope is the published-tag-to-candidate interval, `v0.4.1..main`; this release record does not claim unprovided audit, installer, or CI checks passed.

## Prepared release notes

### Seventeen skills: seven additional workflows

The collection grows from ten skills in v0.4.1 to seventeen. The seven additions are:

- **claim-check** — map claims to evidence, attribution and verdicts; produce `review.md` and `claims.md`.
- **change-impact** — report statically evidenced change impacts, unknown reachability and proposed verification.
- **bug-repro** — match a reported symptom, minimize a trigger with controls, and retain reproducible artifacts.
- **incident-triage** — organize event/source identity, timing uncertainty, impact and an investigation handoff.
- **benchmark-audit** — audit bounded benchmark records and evaluate claims against retained evidence.
- **experiment-design** — freeze a falsifiable experiment plan, controls, matrix, budgets, analysis and execution handoff; it does not run the experiment.
- **verification-loop** — verify a change against supplied criteria, with bounded authorized execution or inspection-only planning and optional recipe qualification.

These are instruction workflows, not guarantees of correct outputs or efficacy. Their permissions differ by task: inspection and report creation do not imply authority to execute targets, install dependencies, access credentials, alter a product, spend resources, publish or merge. Execution-oriented first-use paths require explicit bounded authorization; source/placement checks do not establish native activation, automatic selection or task quality. Existing per-skill scopes and maturity boundaries remain in force, including Refpot preview and repository-readiness experimental status.

The ten existing bundles remain part of the collection: intpot-cli, intpot-python, summonpot, dexpot, refpot-database, summonpot-pr-review, summonpot-code-review, repository-readiness, document-actions and decision-brief. Dexpot is refreshed to SkillPot instruction version 0.3.0, targeting Dexpot v0.8.1 and its documented path-annotation boundary. The newer Dexpot guide raises its minimum product prerequisite; consult that skill's compatibility guidance before use. This upstream-source inspection is not a claim of runtime execution.

### Construction, distribution, documentation and evaluation-scope changes

The implementation interval includes collection-wide construction hardening: validation now covers original workflows as well as imported catalogue packages, including portable frontmatter, duplicate/malformed metadata, required resources, and standalone-package link destinations. Public distribution records describe selected-skill installer checks and their limited filesystem/placement scope; they do not establish native client activation or model/task improvement.

Documentation and first-use guidance distinguish the immutable published v0.4.1 snapshot from current-source workflows, preserve permissions and user-edit cautions, and provide task-specific setup and output boundaries. Website source/build/browser workflow and CI coverage changed in this interval; website deployment remains independent of collection publication. Historical task records and authored fixtures retain their own provenance and limits. No efficacy, accuracy, speed, safety or broad agent-performance claim is made here.
