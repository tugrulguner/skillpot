## Material findings and proposed local edits

- **C5–C9 / ¶2 — High consequence.** The letter supports commissioning only. It explicitly says planned start, with scope and independence to be documented separately, and does not report fieldwork, completion, findings, or a security opinion (S2, `sources.md` ¶2). Thus independence, completion, no-critical-issues finding, and proof of product security are unresolved; do not assert them. Proposed action: retain only “The company commissioned Cedar Audit Group to conduct a review of the AcmeFlow deployment”; verify the separate statement of work and obtain the actual report before stating independence, completion, findings, or security conclusions.
- **C3–C4 / ¶1 — Material scope/causality overreach.** S1 says median processing time was 40% lower in a before/after comparison for 25 customers in its 2025 benchmark; it provides no control group and excludes any identification of Northstar. It does not support all customers, this team, or causation. Proposed edit: attribute the benchmark precisely: “AcmeFlow reports that, across 25 customers in its 2025 benchmark, median processing time was 40% lower with AcmeFlow enabled than in each customer’s prior measurement window.” Remove the causal team conclusion pending team-specific controlled evidence.
- **C10–C13 / ¶3 — Ratio is calculable; rate, increase, causation, and user impact are not.** S3 records 18 tickets and 60 accounts. Executed arithmetic: 18 ÷ 60 × 100 = 30 tickets per 100 included accounts (a count ratio, not an incident rate). No user denominator, baseline/comparison tally, or causal analysis is supplied. Proposed edit: “The worksheet logged 18 incident tickets across 60 accounts during the 20-day pilot.” Do not call this an increase or share of users.
- **C14–C17 / ¶4 — Appendix description is supported.** The worksheet gives 20 days and 18 tickets across 60 accounts and lacks user count, baseline tally, and causal analysis (S3). Proposed local edit: none required; “comparison period” is a reasonable description of the missing baseline, not evidence of a contrary value.
- **C1 / ¶1 — Metadata unresolved.** The appendix does not identify or verify the draft’s stated version/preparation date. Proposed action: verify from draft provenance if material.
