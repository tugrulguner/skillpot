# Provenance

Original SkillPot workflow, authored under the repository's MIT license. It is based on the accepted design in [`docs/experiment-design-proposal.md`](https://github.com/tugrulguner/skillpot/blob/0b637dcb078f692d9b34101f211d06e63d234f7e/docs/experiment-design-proposal.md) (proposal accepted at SkillPot `main` commit `0b637dcb078f692d9b34101f211d06e63d234f7e`). The implementation preserves its task boundary, permissions, destination guard and exact eight-section output contract while making the procedure installable and adding directly linked template, checklist and input-only practice resources.

The separate retrospective fixture at [`examples/experiment-design-proposal/`](https://github.com/tugrulguner/skillpot/tree/0b637dcb078f692d9b34101f211d06e63d234f7e/examples/experiment-design-proposal) remains the accepted authored example. Its expected report and integrity oracle are deliberately excluded from this bundle and user practice inputs. The fixture's retained public source/notice provenance and historical limits remain in its own `provenance.json`; those inputs are not incorporated into this installable instruction package. No private skill material, upstream text/code, model output, new experiment or execution result is incorporated. Maintained with SkillPot source.
